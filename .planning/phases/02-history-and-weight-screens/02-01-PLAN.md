<threat_model>
ASVS L1 / block-high: no new trust boundary is introduced. Treat persisted sessions as untrusted input, never render unsafe assumptions about arrays/dates/template keys, do not log workout content, and never let a presentation component access storage or Firebase. The material integrity risks are mutating a confirmed record before device persistence, silently stripping fields during editing, UTC-shifting a local date, and deleting without confirmation. Copy drafts deeply, spread preserved record fields, write locally before updating React state, and retain UID-scoped Firebase adapters unchanged.
</threat_model>
